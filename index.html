Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

What exactly is a semi-custom kitchen?

+

Semi-custom kitchens use pre-engineered cabinet boxes available in standard sizes (typically 3-inch increments), but allow extensive customization of door styles, finishes, interior storage solutions, and hardware. You get hundreds of design options at 40–60% less than fully custom cabinetry — which is built from scratch to any dimension.

How much will my kitchen remodel cost?

+

Most semi-custom kitchen projects in our market range from $18,000 to $55,000. Key cost factors are: kitchen size, whether any walls or layout are changing, your cabinet line and finish level, and countertop material. We provide a detailed line-item estimate at no charge after your in-home consultation.

How long will my kitchen be out of commission?

+

Active construction — demo through final installation — typically takes 2 to 4 weeks. Before that, expect 2–4 weeks for cabinet lead time after design is finalized. Most homeowners set up a temporary kitchen in another room during this period. We minimize disruption with daily cleanup and clear communication about the schedule.

Do you handle permits?

+

Yes. We pull all required permits for electrical, plumbing, and structural work included in your project. Permit costs are included in your estimate — no surprise fees at the end.

What kind of cabinets do you use?

+

We install KCMA-certified, all-wood cabinets — solid wood box and door construction, no particle board filler. Our lines span modern flat-front, classic shaker, raised-panel traditional, and everything in between, with dozens of finishes from crisp white to warm wood tones to bold painted colors. We'll walk you through every option at your free consultation and help you choose what fits your home and budget perfectly.

Is financing available?

+

Yes. We offer flexible financing options including 12-month same-as-cash and low monthly payment plans. Ask about current rates during your consultation. Most homeowners can get started with as little as $0 down.
